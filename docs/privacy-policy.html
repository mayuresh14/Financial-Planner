Privacy Policy — Finance Planner

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Finance Planner ("the app") is an Android application for personal financial calculators (SIP, PPF, EPF, FD, RD, EMI, and related tools). This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

No account required

The app does not require you to create an account or log in. All calculator inputs (amounts, rates, durations, etc.) are processed entirely on your device and are not sent to us or stored on any server. Your theme, language, and default-rate preferences are also stored only locally on your device.

Analytics and crash reporting

The app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to understand feature usage and diagnose crashes. These services may automatically collect:

  • Device information (model, OS version, language, country)
  • App usage events (e.g. which calculator screens are opened, which buttons are tapped)
  • Crash logs and non-fatal error reports, to help us fix bugs
  • A random, non-personally-identifiable installation identifier

We do not use this data to identify you personally, and we do not sell or share it with third parties beyond Google's role as our analytics/crash reporting provider. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles this data.

No advertising

The app does not show ads and does not use any advertising SDKs.

Data retention

Locally stored preferences remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Analytics and crash data is retained by Firebase per Google's standard retention policies.

Children's privacy

The app is a general financial-planning tool not directed at children, and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app evolves (e.g. when account-based features are introduced in a future release). Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
